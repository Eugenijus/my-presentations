What changed since May

  • The fastest repos are now skills frameworks, not SDD kits. mattpocock/skills adds roughly 1,470 stars a day and obra/superpowers roughly 600. Both sit close to 300k stars and both are SDD-adjacent rather than classic spec toolkits.
  • Spec Kit is the fastest dedicated SDD toolkit, up from 93k to 141.7k at about 157 stars a day over the last week.
  • GSD changed homes. The original repo was archived on June 26 with about 64.4k stars frozen. Development continues as open-gsd/gsd-core, now at 10.7k.
  • Corrections to the May edition: OpenSpec launched in August 2025 (not January), and Pimzino's repo has about 3.8k stars (not ~400). It is now legacy, superseded by spec-workflow-mcp.
